I am an undergraduate student at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT), enrolled in 2024. My research interests lie in computer vision, with a focus on vision foundation models and large multimodal models. My current research focuses on remote sensing image segmentation, with an emphasis on learning boundary details and global structure.

I developed WaveMamba and published it in IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters as a co-first author (with my research mentor), listed second. I independently carried out the method design, implementation, experiments, and manuscript writing. I also lead a university-level undergraduate innovation project on wavelet- and Mamba-based SAR image segmentation.

I received the National Scholarship in 2026. My comprehensive evaluation rank was 11/344 in the 2025–2026 academic year. I welcome research opportunities and discussions in vision foundation models and multimodal learning.
