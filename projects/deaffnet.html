DEAFFNet

Water Body Segmentation Network for SAR Images Combining Dual Encoder and Adaptive Feature Fusion

Bin Han, Xin Huang, Fengyi Li, and Xiaozhen Lu

Acta Geodaetica et Cartographica Sinica, 55(1): 101–113, 2026.

Third author · Experimental data measurement

Research period: November 2025 – January 2026

Overview

DEAFFNet combines local detail and global context for water segmentation in SAR images with complex backgrounds. A lightweight residual encoder and a Swin Transformer encoder extract complementary representations.

Method

The network uses cross-attention to exchange information between the two encoders and adaptive weights to fuse their features. The decoder integrates multi-scale content-aware upsampling to recover water-body details.

My Contribution

I was responsible for experimental data measurement and am the third author of the paper.

Reference

Bin Han, Xin Huang, Fengyi Li, and Xiaozhen Lu. “Water Body Segmentation Network for SAR Images Combining Dual Encoder and Adaptive Feature Fusion.” Acta Geodaetica et Cartographica Sinica, vol. 55, no. 1, pp. 101–113, 2026.
